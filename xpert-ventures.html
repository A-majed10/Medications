How We Work

From first meeting to funded.

We move fast because we know your time is your most precious resource. Our process is designed to respect that.

01

Introductory Call

A 30-minute conversation to understand your vision, traction, and what you're building. No deck required for the first call.

02

Deep Dive

We get hands-on — reviewing your product, market, team, and financials. We ask hard questions because we care about building something real.

03

Partner Meeting

Meet the full Xpert team. This is collaborative, not an interrogation — we want you to evaluate us as much as we evaluate you.

04

Term Sheet

If there's mutual conviction, we move to a term sheet within days. Founder-friendly terms, transparent process, no games.
